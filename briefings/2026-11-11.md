🗓️ Gujarat Daily Briefing | 2026-11-11

**Top Headlines:**
• Union Home Minister Amit Shah inaugurated and laid foundation stones for around 30 development projects worth over ₹1,400 crore, of which about ₹1,024 crore are Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation works (Ahmedabad / Gandhinagar constituency) (Greater Kashmir / Asianet Newsable / ThePrint, Nov 9, 2026)
• Shah set December 31, 2028 as the deadline for a drug-free Gujarat, a year ahead of the national 2029 target, and announced a confidential 1033 helpline for reporting drug sales, to go live on December 1 (Ahmedabad) (Deccan Chronicle / The NewsMill / Webindia123, Nov 9–10, 2026)
• CM Bhupendra Patel began Vikram Samvat 2083 with prayers at Panchdev Temple and urged citizens to build a "Viksit and drug-free Gujarat" on the Gujarati New Year (Gandhinagar) (ANI / Asianet Newsable, Nov 10, 2026)
• IFSCA issued a consultation paper proposing that registered research entities in GIFT City be allowed to offer model portfolios (GIFT City, Gandhinagar) (Business Standard, Nov 9, 2026)

**Key Sections:**
🏛️ Politics & Governance — Amit Shah, on a Diwali–New Year visit to the state, set a December 31, 2028 target for making Gujarat drug-free, ahead of the Centre's December 31, 2029 deadline for India, and announced a toll-free 1033 helpline and control room, starting December 1, through which citizens can report drug sales or use without revealing their identity (Deccan Chronicle; The NewsMill; Webindia123; Asianet Newsable, Nov 9–10, 2026). CM Bhupendra Patel marked the Gujarati New Year on November 10 with prayers and rituals at Panchdev Temple in Gandhinagar (ANI; Asianet Newsable, Nov 10, 2026).

💼 Business & Economy — The International Financial Services Centres Authority released a consultation paper on November 9 proposing that registered research entities in GIFT City may offer model portfolios, with broker-dealers and other entities required to register as research entities first and with mandatory risk disclosures proposed (Business Standard, Nov 9, 2026).

🏗️ Infrastructure & Development — On November 9, Amit Shah inaugurated and laid foundation stones for around 30 civic and infrastructure projects worth over ₹1,400 crore in Ahmedabad; about ₹1,024 crore of the works are being carried out by the Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation in his Gandhinagar Lok Sabha constituency, with the rest through the state Roads and Buildings Department and the Centre (Greater Kashmir; Asianet Newsable; ThePrint; Deccan Chronicle, Nov 9, 2026).

🌍 Local News & Culture — Gujarat celebrated the start of Vikram Samvat 2083 on November 10, with temples including Dakor, Ambaji and Dwarka holding annakut darshan (Sandesh, Nov 10, 2026).

**Brief Summary:**
Amit Shah's visit dominated the window, combining a ₹1,400 crore Ahmedabad development package with a 2028 drug-free deadline for Gujarat and a new 1033 helpline. The Gujarati New Year (Vikram Samvat 2083) set the backdrop, with CM Bhupendra Patel echoing the drug-free message, while IFSCA advanced GIFT City's regulatory framework with a model-portfolio proposal.
