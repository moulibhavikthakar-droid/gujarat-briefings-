**Key Sections:**
🏛️ Politics & Governance — The Gujarat State Election Commission's Model Code of Conduct remains in force for the Gandhinagar Municipal Corporation election after nomination filing closed on September 26; BJP completed candidate lists for all 44 seats across 11 wards, while Congress and AAP also filed full or near-full slates. Scrutiny of nominations is scheduled for September 28, with withdrawal until September 29, voting on October 11 and results on October 13. (Gujarat Samachar, Punjab Kesari)

💼 Business & Economy — The United Forum of Bank Unions' three-day nationwide strike over a five-day banking week demand began September 28; SLBC-Gujarat issued an advisory urging customers statewide to complete cash, cheque and paperwork-related transactions beforehand, while digital channels (ATMs, UPI, net banking) are expected to keep functioning. Separately, Aluminium Bharat 2026 — organised by the Aluminium Extrusion Manufacturers Association of India (ALEMAI) — opened September 26 at Gandhinagar's Helipad Exhibition Centre and runs through September 29, spread across more than 30,000 square metres with 500+ exhibitors from the aluminium value chain. (Indian Masterminds, Gujarat Samachar, ALEMAI, Khabarpatri)

🏗️ Infrastructure & Development — The Union Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways notified Deendayal Port (Kandla) and Mundra Port, along with JNPA and Paradip, as India's first "mega ports" under Section 73 of the Indian Ports Act, 2025, per a September 25 notification reported September 26; the designation carries cargo thresholds of 150 MMT or 7.5 million TEUs and does not itself alter a port's major/non-major status. (DeshGujarat, ETV Bharat, Business Standard, Maritime Gateway)

🌍 Local News & Culture — The seven-day Bhadarvi Poonam Mahamela at the Ambaji Shaktipeeth temple in Banaskantha district reached its main full-moon day on September 26; Sandesh reported cumulative attendance climbing into the tens of lakhs over the week, with more than 3.72 lakh darshans and over ₹1.83 crore in temple-trust collections recorded on the mela's sixth day alone. (Sandesh, Organiser)

**Brief Summary:**
Gujarat's news over this window centered on governance and economic milestones: Gandhinagar's civic election entered its scrutiny phase as a national bank strike began affecting branches statewide, while the Centre's mega-port notification gave Kandla and Mundra new national infrastructure status. Gandhinagar also hosted a major industrial exhibition even as the state's largest annual pilgrimage fair reached its peak day at Ambaji.
